Our newest book and the one we use ourselves for a year at a time. Fifteen signatures of 120 gsm dot grid paper are sewn together, rounded, lined with mull and cased into boards covered in Belgian linen. It is a proper hardback: it protects the pages in a bag and stands on a shelf with the others.

The 5 mm dot grid is printed in a light warm grey that disappears under writing and stays visible enough to draw a straight table. Pages are numbered, and there are two index pages at the front.

A red ribbon marker keeps your place. The linen softens and takes on a slight sheen with handling, which is the point of linen.
