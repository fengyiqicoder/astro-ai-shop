The kit we wish we had when we started. It has everything to sew three small Coptic-stitch notebooks: pre-cut paper, covers already wrapped in bookcloth, waxed linen thread in three colors, a needle, an awl and a bone folder.

The printed guide walks through folding, punching and sewing with a photo for every step, and ends with three variations once you have the basic stitch. It is written by us, the same way we teach the stitch at workshops.
