Three slim notebooks for the pocket, sewn with a three-station saddle stitch in cotton thread. We print nothing on the covers except a small panel you can write a date or a project name on, so they stay plain until you use them.

The set mixes rulings on purpose: dot grid for lists and quick sketches, lined for writing, blank for everything else. Each one has 48 pages, enough for a few weeks of daily notes.

Covers are recycled kraft card with rounded corners so they do not dog-ear in a pocket. A colored thread band on the spine tells them apart at a glance.
