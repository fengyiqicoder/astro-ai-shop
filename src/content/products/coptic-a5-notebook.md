This is the notebook we started the bindery with. Six signatures of 100 gsm wove paper are folded, pierced and sewn by hand onto the covers with a chain stitch you can see along the spine. Because nothing holds the spine but thread, the book opens completely flat, which makes it good for drawing across a spread, writing at a narrow desk or laying open next to a keyboard.

The covers are 2 mm greyboard wrapped in cotton bookcloth. They are stiff enough to write on in your lap and soften slightly at the corners over the first few months.

Each one is sewn after you order, which takes us a few days. The paper is blank, acid-free and takes pencil, ballpoint, gel and most fountain pen inks well. Light watercolor washes buckle the page a little; it is not a sketchbook for wet media.
