A bone folder is the binder's most used tool and the cheapest one to get right. This one is 150 mm long, with a pointed end for scoring and getting into corners and a rounded end for creasing and smoothing.

It comes polished but not coated, so it grips paper slightly instead of skating over it. It gets smoother and more pleasant to hold the more you use it.
