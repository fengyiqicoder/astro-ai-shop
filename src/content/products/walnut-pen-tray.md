The tray is cut from a single piece of black walnut and shaped on a router, then sanded by hand to 320 grit and finished with hardwax oil. The well is deep enough that a round pen will not roll out when you knock the desk.

A thin cork base keeps it from sliding and from scratching the desk under it. It is made for us in small batches by a woodworker in our neighborhood, from offcuts of furniture-grade boards.
